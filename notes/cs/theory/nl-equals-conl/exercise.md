::: {.problem #prob-sipser3e-8-q27 origin="external" source="sipser3e-8-q27"}
Recall that a directed graph is *strongly connected* if every two nodes are connected by a directed path in each direction. Let
$$\mathit{STRONGLY\text-CONNECTED}=\{\langle G\rangle\mid G \text{ is a strongly connected graph}\}.$$
Show that $\mathit{STRONGLY\text-CONNECTED}$ is NL-complete.
:::
