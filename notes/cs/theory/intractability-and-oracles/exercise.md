::: {.problem #prob-sipser3e-9-q19 origin="external" source="sipser3e-9-q19"}
Define the *unique-sat problem* to be
$$\mathit{USAT}=\{\langle\phi\rangle\mid \phi \text{ is a Boolean formula that has a single satisfying assignment}\}.$$
Show that $\mathit{USAT}\in\mathrm P^{\mathit{SAT}}$.
:::
