::: {.problem #prob-sipser3e-8-q22 origin="external" source="sipser3e-8-q22"}
a. Let $\mathit{ADD}=\{\langle x,y,z\rangle\mid x,y,z>0 \text{ are binary integers and } x+y=z\}$. Show that $\mathit{ADD}\in\mathrm L$.

b. Let $\mathit{PAL\text-ADD}=\{\langle x,y\rangle\mid x,y>0 \text{ are binary integers where } x+y \text{ is an integer whose binary representation is a palindrome}\}$. (Note that the binary representation of the sum is assumed not to have leading zeros. A palindrome is a string that equals its reverse.) Show that $\mathit{PAL\text-ADD}\in\mathrm L$.
:::
