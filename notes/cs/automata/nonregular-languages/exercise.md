::: {.problem #prob-sipser3e-1-q53 origin="external" source="sipser3e-1-q53"}
Let $\Sigma=\{0,1,+,=\}$ and
$$\mathit{ADD}=\{x{=}y{+}z\mid x,y,z \text{ are binary integers, and } x \text{ is the sum of } y \text{ and } z\}.$$
Show that $\mathit{ADD}$ is not regular.
:::
