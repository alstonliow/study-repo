**A Simple Simplex Example**

Consider a linear program (LP) consisting of two variables $x_1$ and $x_2$ satisfying the following three constraints:
$$x_1+x_2\le10,\qquad x_2\ge4x_1-20,\qquad x_1+3x_2\le24,\qquad x_1,x_2\ge0.$$
The goal is to maximize the value of the objective function $p=4x_1+x_2$.

(a) Draw a diagram of the feasible region.

(b) Write the given LP in standard form, and transform this standard form representation into slack form.

(c) Use Simplex to solve the resulting slack form LP. Identify the pivots you choose and give the resulting modified LPs and the successive feasible solutions. Indicate the successive solutions on your diagram from Part (a).

(d) Give the dual LP of your standard-form LP from Part (b) and give its optimal value. (Hint: Use your solution to Part (c).)
