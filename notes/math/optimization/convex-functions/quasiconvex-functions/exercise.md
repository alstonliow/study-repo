::: {.problem #prob-ee364ahw-2-q3 origin="external" source="ee364ahw-2-q3"}
For each of the following functions determine whether it is convex, concave, quasiconvex, or quasiconcave.

(a) $f(x)=e^x-1$ on $\mathbf{R}$.

(b) $f(x_1,x_2)=x_1x_2$ on $\mathbf{R}^2_{++}$.

(c) $f(x_1,x_2)=1/(x_1x_2)$ on $\mathbf{R}^2_{++}$.

(d) $f(x_1,x_2)=x_1/x_2$ on $\mathbf{R}^2_{++}$.

(e) $f(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2/x_2$ on $\mathbf{R}\times\mathbf{R}_{++}$.

(f) $f(x_1,x_2)=x_1^\alpha x_2^{1-\alpha}$, where $0\le\alpha\le1$, on $\mathbf{R}^2_{++}$.
:::
