Recall $K^*=\{y\mid y^Tx\ge0\ \text{for all}\ x\in K\}$ (@def-dual-cone).

(a) Every $y$ satisfies $y^T0\ge0$, so $K^*=\mathbf{R}^2$.

(b) $y^Tx\ge0$ for all $x$ forces $y=0$ (take $x=-y$), so $K^*=\{0\}$.

(c) $K$ is generated by $(1,1)$ and $(-1,1)$, so $y\in K^*$ iff $y_1+y_2\ge0$ and $-y_1+y_2\ge0$, that is, $K^*=\{(y_1,y_2)\mid\lvert y_1\rvert\le y_2\}=K$: the cone is self-dual (the absolute value on $\mathbf{R}$ is its own dual norm, @exm-dual-cones).

(d) $K$ is the subspace spanned by $(1,-1)$. If $y^Tx\ge0$ for $x$ and for $-x$ then $y^Tx=0$, so $K^*$ is the orthogonal complement, $K^*=\{(y_1,y_2)\mid y_1=y_2\}$.
:::
