*Voronoi description of halfspace.* Let $a$ and $b$ be distinct points in $\mathbf{R}^n$. Show that the set of all points that are closer (in Euclidean norm) to $a$ than $b$, i.e., $\{x\mid\lVert x-a\rVert_2\le\lVert x-b\rVert_2\}$, is a halfspace. Describe it explicitly as an inequality of the form $c^Tx\le d$. Draw a picture.
