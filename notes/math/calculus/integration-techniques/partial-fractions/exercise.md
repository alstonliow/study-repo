a) Derive a formula for $\int\sec x\,dx$ by writing $\sec x=\dfrac{\cos x}{1-\sin^2x}$ (verify this), and then making a substitution for $\sin x$ and using partial fractions. (Your final answer must be expressed in terms of $x$.)

b) Convert the formula into the more familiar one by multiplying the fraction in the answer on both top and bottom by $1+\sin x$. (Note that $(1/2)\ln u=\ln\sqrt u$.)
