Sketch five level curves for each of the following functions. Also, for a-d, sketch the portion of the graph of the function lying in the first octant; include in your sketch the traces of the graph in the three coordinate planes, if possible.

a) $1-x-y$  b) $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$  c) $x^2+y^2$  d) $1-x^2-y^2$  e) $x^2-y^2$
