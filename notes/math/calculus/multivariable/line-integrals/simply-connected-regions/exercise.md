Find the flux of the vector field $\vec F=\dfrac x{r^2}\,\hat{\imath}+\dfrac y{r^2}\,\hat{\jmath}$ outwards through any circle centered at $(1,0)$ of radius $a\ne1$. Consider the cases $a>1$ and $a<1$ separately. Explain your answers with diagrams.

Optional: use the applet on the course web page to get a better understanding of what happens when $a$ changes from a value lower than 1 to a value greater than 1.

*Note (these notes):* The optional applet part has nothing to hand in.
