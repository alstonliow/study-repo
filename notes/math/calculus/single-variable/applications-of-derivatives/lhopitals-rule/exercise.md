::: {.problem #prob-mit1801n-6a-q5 origin="external" source="mit1801n-6a-q5"}
By repeated use of L'Hospital's rule,
$$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x^2-4x}{2x-x^2}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{6x-4}{2-2x}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac6{-2}=-3,$$
yet when $x\simeq0$, $\dfrac{3x^2-4x}{2x-x^2}\simeq\dfrac{-4x}{2x}=-2$. Resolve the contradiction.
:::
