Find the ratio $y/x$ which gives the strongest beam.

b) A utility company has a small power plant that can produce $x$ kilowatt hours of electricity daily at a cost of $10-x/10^5$ cents each for $0\le x\le8\times10^5$. Consumers will use $10^5(10-p/2)$ kilowatt hours of electricity daily at a price of $p$ cents per kilowatt hour. What price should the utility charge to maximize its profit?
