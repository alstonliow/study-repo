Do the following series converge or diverge? Give reason. If the series converges, find its sum.

a) $1+\dfrac14+\dfrac1{16}+\dfrac1{64}+\ldots+\dfrac1{4^n}+\ldots$  b) $1-1+1-1+\ldots+(-1)^n+\ldots$  c) $1+\dfrac12+\dfrac23+\ldots+\dfrac{n}{n+1}+\ldots$

*Note (these notes):* Parts (a), (b) and (c) were assigned.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-mit1801n-7b-q1 origin="external" source="mit1801n-7b-q1"}
Using the integral test, tell whether the following series converge or diverge; show work or reasoning.

a) $\displaystyle\sum_0^\infty\frac{n}{n^2+4}$  b) $\displaystyle\sum_0^\infty\frac1{n^2+1}$  f) $\displaystyle\sum_1^\infty\frac1{n^p}$

(In the last two, the answer depends on the value of the parameter $p$.)

*Note (these notes):* Parts (a), (b) and (f) were assigned.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-mit1801n-7b-q2 origin="external" source="mit1801n-7b-q2"}
Using the limit comparison test, tell whether each series converges or diverges; show work or reasoning. (For some of them, simple comparison works.)

a) $\displaystyle\sum_1^\infty\frac1{n^2+3n}$  c) $\displaystyle\sum_1^\infty\frac1{\sqrt{n^2+n}}$  d) $\displaystyle\sum_1^\infty\sin\Big(\frac1{n^2}\Big)$  e) $\displaystyle\sum_1^\infty\frac{\sqrt n}{n^2+1}$

*Note (these notes):* Parts (a), (c), (d) and (e) were assigned.
