Since $\cos\frac\pi2=0$, the quotient is $\dfrac{\cos x-\cos(\pi/2)}{x-\pi/2}$, a difference quotient of $\cos$ at $\pi/2$. Its limit is $(\cos)'(\pi/2)=-\sin\frac\pi2=-1$.
:::
