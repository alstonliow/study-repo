::: {.problem #prob-mit1801f06-1-q7 origin="external" source="mit1801f06-1-q7"}
a) If $u$, $v$ and $w$ are differentiable functions, find the formula for the derivative of their product, $D(uvw)$.

b) Generalize your work in part (a) by guessing the formula for $D(u_1u_2\cdots u_n)$—the derivative of the product of $n$ differentiable functions.

Then prove your formula by mathematical induction (i.e., prove its truth for the product of $n+1$ functions, assuming its truth for the product of $n$ functions).
