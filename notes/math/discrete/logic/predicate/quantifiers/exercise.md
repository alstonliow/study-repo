$\mathrm{elvish}(X)$: $X$ knows Elvish.

(a) Write a universal statement meaning “Nobody knows both Prolog and
    Elvish.”

(b) Suppose (a) is False. Starting with its negation, derive an
    existential statement meaning that someone knows both languages.
