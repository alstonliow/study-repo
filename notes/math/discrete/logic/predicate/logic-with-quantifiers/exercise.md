::: {.problem #prob-predicate-02 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-5.16-q2"}
Predicates: $\mathrm{prolog}(X)$: $X$ knows Prolog;
$\mathrm{elvish}(X)$: $X$ knows Elvish.

(a) Write a universal statement meaning “Nobody knows both Prolog and
    Elvish.”

(b) Suppose (a) is False. Starting with its negation, derive an
    existential statement meaning that someone knows both languages.
:::

(a) Prove that $\exists X\,(P(X)\wedge Q(X))$ implies
    $(\exists X\,P(X))\wedge(\exists X\,Q(X))$.

(b) Prove that $\forall X\,(P(X)\vee Q(X))$ is implied by
    $(\forall X\,P(X))\vee(\forall X\,Q(X))$ in two ways: (i) directly;
    (ii) using (a) and the relationship between existential and
    universal quantifiers.
:::
