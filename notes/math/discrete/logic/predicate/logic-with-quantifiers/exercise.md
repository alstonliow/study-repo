::: {.problem #prob-predicate-14 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-5.16-q14"}
(a) Prove that $\exists X\,(P(X)\wedge Q(X))$ implies
    $(\exists X\,P(X))\wedge(\exists X\,Q(X))$.

(b) Prove that $\forall X\,(P(X)\vee Q(X))$ is implied by
    $(\forall X\,P(X))\vee(\forall X\,Q(X))$ in two ways: (i) directly;
    (ii) using (a) and the relationship between existential and
    universal quantifiers.
