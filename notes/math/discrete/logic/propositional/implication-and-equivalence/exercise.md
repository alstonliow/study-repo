::: {.problem #prob-propositional-06 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-4.21-q6"}
Prove that $(P_1\wedge\cdots\wedge P_n)\Rightarrow C$ is logically equivalent
to $\neg P_1\vee\cdots\vee\neg P_n\vee C$ (a *Horn clause*).
:::

::: {.problem #prob-propositional-09 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-4.21-q9"}
Does logically negating an implication reverse it? Are
$\neg(P\Rightarrow Q)$ and $P\Leftarrow Q$ logically equivalent? If so, prove
it; if not, determine (with proof) if either implies the other.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-propositional-11 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-4.21-q11"}
Prove that

(a) $P\oplus Q=(P\wedge\neg Q)\vee(\neg P\wedge Q)$.

(b) $P\oplus Q=(P\vee Q)\wedge(\neg P\vee\neg Q)$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-propositional-12 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-4.21-q12"}
(a) Express $P\vee Q$ using just $\oplus$ and $\wedge$, and no negation.

(b) Express $P\wedge Q$ using just $\oplus$ and $\vee$, and no negation.
:::
