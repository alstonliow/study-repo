- True for all combinations of $P$ and $Q$ (Tautology)
- False for all combinations of $P$ and $Q$ (Contradiction)
- Neither

(c) $P \land (P \implies \lnot Q) \land (Q)$

(d) $(\lnot P \implies Q) \implies (\lnot Q \implies P)$

(e) $(\lnot P \implies \lnot Q) \land (P \implies \lnot Q) \land (Q)$

(f) $(\lnot(P \land Q)) \land (P \lor Q)$
