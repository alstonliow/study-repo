Let $V$ (“vertices”) and $E$ (“edges”) be finite sets. Suppose $v,w,z$
are variables with domain $V$; $e,f$ are variables with domain $E$; and
$\mathrm{incident}(v,e)$ is a binary predicate meaning vertex $v$ is incident
with edge $e$. Write predicate logic expressions meaning:

(a) Every edge is incident with at least one vertex.

(b) No edge is incident with exactly one vertex (no loops).

(c) Every edge is incident with exactly two vertices.

(d) There are no multiple edges.

(e) $(V,E)$ represents a simple graph.

(f) There are no isolated vertices.
:::
