::: {#exm-crt-small .example name="Solving two congruences" level="easy"}
Find $x$ with $x\equiv2\pmod3$ and $x\equiv3\pmod5$. Here $5^{-1}\equiv2\pmod3$ and $3^{-1}\equiv2\pmod5$, so $u=5\cdot2=10$ and $v=3\cdot2=6$. Then $x=(2\cdot10+3\cdot6)\bmod15=38\bmod15=8$. Check: $8=2\cdot3+2$ and $8=5+3$.
:::
