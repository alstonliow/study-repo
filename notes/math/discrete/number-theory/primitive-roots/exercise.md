::: {.problem #prob-number-theory-17 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-7.19-q17"}
Investigate the primitive roots of 11.

(a) If $a$ is a primitive root of 11, what is the least $k$ such that
    $a^k\equiv1\pmod{11}$?

(b) Show that 2 is a primitive root of 11 by working out its powers
    $2^i$ (multiplying by 2 and reducing mod 11 as you go).

(c) Among the exponents $i$ in your list from (b), which are coprime to
    $\varphi(11)$?

(d) Using (c), list all the primitive roots of 11.
