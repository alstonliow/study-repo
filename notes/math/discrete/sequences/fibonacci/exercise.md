The *Lucas sequence* is defined by
$$l_1=1,\quad l_2=3,\quad l_n=l_{n-1}+l_{n-2}\ (n\ge3).$$
Adapt the approach of §6.7.4 to develop a formula for $l_n$, and then prove
by induction that it works.
