::: {#exm-roots-mod-five .example name="A root modulo 5" level="easy"}
$2x+3\equiv0\pmod5$ gives $2x\equiv2$, and multiplying by $2^{-1}\equiv3$ gives $x\equiv1$: one root, as @thm-at-most-d-roots allows for degree $1$. The "lines" $2x+3$ and $3x-2$ modulo $5$ meet in exactly one point, $(0,3)$.
:::
