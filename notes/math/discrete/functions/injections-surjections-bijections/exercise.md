::: {.problem #prob-functions-08 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-2.19-q8"}
A *bitstring* is a member of $\{0,1\}^*$.

(a) Give a bijection from $\{0,1\}^*$ to $\mathbb{N}$.

Let $\{0,1\}^{*+}$ denote the set of all finite nonempty tuples of
bitstrings, e.g. $(011,1,10001)$, $(10,10,10,10)$, $(\epsilon,1011)$,
$(1010010001)$.

(b) (Challenge) Give a bijection from $\{0,1\}^{*+}$ to $\{0,1\}^*$.
