Here $p(s)=s+1$ has the simple root $r=-1$ and $p'(-1)=1$. The resonant response formula (@thm-generalized-erf) gives $x_p=\dfrac{te^{-t}}{p'(-1)}=te^{-t}$, so the general solution is $x=(t+C)e^{-t}$.
