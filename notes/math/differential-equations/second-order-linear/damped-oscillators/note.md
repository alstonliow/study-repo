An unforced mass–spring–dashpot system $mx''+bx'+kx=0$ (@exm-spring-mass-dashpot) always comes to rest when $b>0$. *How* it comes to rest depends on the sign of the discriminant of its characteristic polynomial.

::: {#def-damping-regimes .definition name="Underdamped, critically damped, overdamped"}
For $m,k>0$ and $b\ge0$, the characteristic roots of $ms^2+bs+k$ are $\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4mk}}{2m}$. The system is

- *undamped* if $b=0$: roots $\pm i\omega_0$ with *natural frequency* $\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$;
- *underdamped* if $0<b^2<4mk$: complex roots $-\tfrac{b}{2m}\pm i\omega_d$ with *damped frequency* $\omega_d=\sqrt{\omega_0^2-\tfrac{b^2}{4m^2}}$;
- *critically damped* if $b^2=4mk$: one repeated root $-\tfrac{b}{2m}$;
- *overdamped* if $b^2>4mk$: two distinct negative real roots.
:::

::: {#prp-damped-solutions .proposition name="Behaviour in each regime"}
Let $b>0$. Every solution of $mx''+bx'+kx=0$ tends to $0$. In the underdamped case the solutions are
$$
x = A\,e^{-bt/2m}\cos(\omega_dt-\phi),
$$
oscillations with *pseudo-period* $2\pi/\omega_d$ inside the decaying envelope $\pm Ae^{-bt/2m}$. In the critically damped and overdamped cases a nonzero solution crosses $x=0$ at most once.
:::

::: {.proof}
1. In the underdamped case @thm-second-order-characteristic-roots gives $x=e^{-bt/2m}(c_1\cos\omega_dt+c_2\sin\omega_dt)$, and @prp-sinusoid-identity rewrites the bracket as $A\cos(\omega_dt-\phi)$.

2. In the critically damped case $x=(c_1+c_2t)e^{rt}$ with $r<0$; it vanishes only where $c_1+c_2t=0$, at most once.

3. In the overdamped case $x=c_1e^{r_1t}+c_2e^{r_2t}$ with $r_1\neq r_2$ negative; $x=0$ means $e^{(r_1-r_2)t}=-c_2/c_1$, which has at most one solution because the exponential is monotone.

4. In all cases each term contains a factor $e^{\alpha t}$ with $\alpha<0$ (times a polynomial or a bounded function), so $x\to0$.
:::

::: {#exm-three-damping-regimes .example name="Three dashpots on one spring"}
Take $m=1$, $k=4$, $x(0)=1$, $x'(0)=0$.

1. $b=1$ (underdamped): roots $-\tfrac12\pm i\tfrac{\sqrt{15}}2$, and $x=e^{-t/2}\left(\cos\tfrac{\sqrt{15}}2t+\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{15}}\sin\tfrac{\sqrt{15}}2t\right)$.
2. $b=4$ (critical): root $-2$ twice, and $x=(1+2t)e^{-2t}$.
3. $b=5$ (overdamped): roots $-1,-4$, and $x=\tfrac43e^{-t}-\tfrac13e^{-4t}$.

The critically damped solution returns to rest fastest without oscillating; the overdamped one is slowed by its slow mode $e^{-t}$.
