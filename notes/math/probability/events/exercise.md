::: {.problem #prob-probability-1-08 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-9.10-q8"}
Prove that, for any events $A$ and $B$,
$$\Pr(A\cup B) = 1-\Pr(\overline A\cap\overline B).$$
:::

::: {.problem #prob-probability-1-09 origin="external" source="fit1058-cn-9.10-q9"}
Prove by induction on $n$ that, for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$ and any mutually disjoint
sets $A_1,\dots,A_n$,
$$\Pr(A_1\sqcup A_2\sqcup\cdots\sqcup A_n) = \sum_{k=1}^n\Pr(A_k).$$
:::
