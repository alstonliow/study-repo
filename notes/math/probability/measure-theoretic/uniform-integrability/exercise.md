::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-4-q6 origin="external" source="camii-pms-4-q6"}
Let $(X_n:n\in\mathbb N)$ be an identically distributed sequence in $L^2(\mathbb P)$. Show that
$$n\,\mathbb P\big(|X_1|>\varepsilon\sqrt n\big)\to0$$
as $n\to\infty$, for all $\varepsilon>0$. Deduce that $n^{-1/2}\max_{k\le n}|X_k|\to0$ in probability.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-6-q1 origin="external" source="camii-pms-6-q1"}
Find a uniformly integrable sequence of random variables $(X_n:n\in\mathbb N)$ such that both $X_n\to0$ a.s. and $\mathbb E\sup_n|X_n|=\infty$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-6-q2 origin="external" source="camii-pms-6-q2"}
Let $(X_n:n\in\mathbb N)$ be an identically distributed sequence in $L^2(\mathbb P)$. Show that
$$\frac{\mathbb E\big(\max_{k\le n}|X_k|\big)}{\sqrt n}\to0\qquad\text{as }n\to\infty.$$
:::
