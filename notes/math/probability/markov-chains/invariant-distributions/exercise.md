**Moving Books Arround.** You have $N$ books on your shelf, labelled $1,2,\dots,N$. You pick a book $j$ with probability $1/N$. Then you place it on the left of all others on the shelf. You repeat the process, independently. Construct a Markov chain which takes values in the set of all $N!$ permutations of the books.

(a) Find the transition probabilities of the Markov chain.

(b) Find its stationary distribution.

Hint: You can guess the stationary distribution before computing it.
