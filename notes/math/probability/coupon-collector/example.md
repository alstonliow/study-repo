::: {#exm-cereal-cards .example name="Cards in cereal boxes" level="easy"}
Each cereal box holds one of $n=100$ different cards, each equally likely. By @thm-coupon-collector, collecting all of them takes on average $100H_{100}\approx519$ boxes. The approximation $H_n\approx\ln n+0.5772$ gives $100(\ln100+0.5772)\approx518$.
:::
