::: {.problem #prob-camib-sts-1-q5 origin="external" source="camib-sts-1-q5"}
Let $X_1,\dots,X_n\overset{\text{iid}}{\sim}\mathrm{Unif}(0,\theta)$. Find the maximum likelihood estimator $\hat\theta$ of $\theta$. By considering the distribution of $\hat\theta/\theta$ and for $\alpha\in(0,1)$, find an appropriate, one-sided $100(1-\alpha)\%$ confidence interval for $\theta$ based on $\hat\theta$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camib-sts-1-q6 origin="external" source="camib-sts-1-q6"}
Suppose that $X_1\sim N(\theta_1,1)$ and $X_2\sim N(\theta_2,1)$ independently, where $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ are unknown. Show that both the square $S$ and the circle $C$ in $\mathbb R^2$ given by
$$S=\{(\theta_1,\theta_2):|\theta_1-X_1|\le2.236,\ |\theta_2-X_2|\le2.236\},$$
$$C=\{(\theta_1,\theta_2):(\theta_1-X_1)^2+(\theta_2-X_2)^2\le5.991\}$$
are $95\%$ confidence sets for $(\theta_1,\theta_2)$. Hint: $\Phi(2.236)=(1+\sqrt{0.95})/2$ where $\Phi$ is the distribution function of a $N(0,1)$ random variable. What might be a sensible criterion for choosing between $S$ and $C$?
:::
