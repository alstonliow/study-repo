::: {.problem #prob-camib-sts-3-q3 origin="external" source="camib-sts-3-q3"}
Let $X\sim N_n(\mu,\sigma^2I)$ where $I$ is the $n\times n$ identity matrix, and let $P$ be an $n\times n$ orthogonal projection matrix; i.e. $P^2=P=P^{\mathsf T}$. Show that the random vectors $PX$ and $(I-P)X$ are independent.
:::
