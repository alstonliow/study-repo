::: {.problem #prob-camib-sts-2-q6 origin="external" source="camib-sts-2-q6"}
A random sample of $59$ people from the planet Krypton yielded the results below.

| | | Eye-colour | |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | Blue | Brown |
| Sex | Male | 19 | 10 |
| | Female | 9 | 21 |

Carry out a Pearson's chi-squared test at the $5\%$ level of the null hypothesis that sex and eye-colour are independent factors on Krypton. Now carry out the corresponding test at the $5\%$ level of the null hypothesis that each of the cell probabilities is equal to $1/4$. Comment on your results.
:::

| Placebo | 18 | 17 | 15 |
| Half dose | 20 | 10 | 20 |
