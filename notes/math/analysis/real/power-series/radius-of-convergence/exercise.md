Find the radius of convergence of each of the following power series.
$$\text{(i)} \sum_{n\geqslant0}\frac{2\cdot4\cdot6\cdots(2n+2)}{1\cdot4\cdot7\cdots(3n+1)}z^n, \quad \text{(ii)} \sum_{n\geqslant1}\frac{z^{3n}}{n2^n}, \quad \text{(iii)} \sum_{n\geqslant0}\frac{n^nz^n}{n!}, \quad \text{(iv)} \sum_{n\geqslant1}n^{\sqrt n}z^n$$
