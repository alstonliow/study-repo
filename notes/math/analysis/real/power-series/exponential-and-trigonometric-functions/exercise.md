::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-4-q9 origin="external" source="cama1s-4-q9"}
Recall the properties of sine and cosine from lectures. For $x \in \mathbb{R}$, set $\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$.

(a) Find the derivative of $\tan x$.

(b) Justify that there is a differentiable inverse function $\tan^{-1}x$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$. What is its derivative?

(c) Now let $g(x) = x - x^3/3 + x^5/5 + \cdots$ for $|x| < 1$. By considering $g'(x)$, explain carefully why $\tan^{-1}x = g(x)$ for $|x| < 1$.
