::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-1-q13 origin="external" source="cama1s-1-q13"}
Consider the two series
$$1 - \frac12 + \frac13 - \frac14 + \frac15 - \frac16 + \cdots \qquad\text{and}\qquad 1 + \frac13 - \frac12 + \frac15 + \frac17 - \frac14 + \cdots,$$
having the same terms but taken in a different order. Let $s_n$ and $t_n$ be the corresponding partial sums up to $n$ terms.

(a) Show that $s_{2n} = h_{2n} - h_n$ and $t_{3n} = h_{4n} - \frac12 h_{2n} - \frac12 h_n$, where $h_n = \sum_{k \le n} \frac1k$.

(b) Show that $s_n$ converges to a limit $s > 0$ and that $t_n$ converges to $3s/2$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-1-q14 origin="external" source="cama1s-1-q14"}
Let $(x_n)$ and $(y_n)$ be real sequences with $x_n \to 0$ and $y_n \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$.

(a) Show that there is a sequence $(\sigma_n)$ of signs (i.e. $\sigma_n \in \{-1, +1\}$ for all $n$) such that $\sum \sigma_nx_n$ is convergent.

(b) ($\star\star$) Must there be a sequence $(\sigma_n)$ of signs such that $\sum \sigma_nx_n$ and $\sum \sigma_ny_n$ are both convergent?
:::
