::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-4-q10 origin="external" source="cama1s-4-q10"}
The *infinite product* $\prod_{n=1}^\infty(1 + a_n)$ is said to *converge* if the sequence of partial products $(p_N)_N$ with $p_N = (1 + a_1)\cdots(1 + a_N)$ converges.

(a) Suppose that $a_n \geqslant 0$ for all $n$. Putting $s_m = a_1 + \cdots + a_m$, prove that $s_N \leqslant p_N \leqslant e^{s_N}$, and deduce that $\prod_{n=1}^\infty(1 + a_n)$ converges if and only if $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$ converges.

(b) Evaluate $\prod_{n=2}^\infty(1 + 1/(n^2 - 1))$.
:::
