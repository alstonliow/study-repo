::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-2-q3 origin="external" source="cama1s-2-q3"}
Let $f, g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be such that $f(x) \to \ell$ as $x \to a$ and $g(y) \to k$ as $y \to \ell$. Must it be true that $g(f(x)) \to k$ as $x \to a$?
:::

::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-2-q11 origin="external" source="cama1s-2-q11"}
Let $f(x) = x + 2x^2\sin(1/x)$ for $x \neq 0$ and $f(0) = 0$. Show that $f$ is differentiable everywhere and that $f'(0) = 1$, but that there is no interval around $0$ on which $f$ is increasing.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-cama1s-3-q6 origin="external" source="cama1s-3-q6"}
Given $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$, define $f_\alpha : [-1,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ by $f_\alpha(x) = |x|^\alpha\sin(1/x)$ for $x \neq 0$ and $f_\alpha(0) = 0$. Is $f_0$ continuous? Is $f_1$ differentiable? Draw a table, with 9 columns labelled $\alpha = -\frac12, 0, \frac12, 1, \frac32, 2, \frac52, 3, \frac72$ and with 6 rows labelled "$f_\alpha$ bounded", "$f_\alpha$ continuous", "$f_\alpha$ differentiable", "$f'_\alpha$ bounded", "$f'_\alpha$ continuous", "$f'_\alpha$ differentiable". Place ticks and crosses at appropriate places in the table.
:::
