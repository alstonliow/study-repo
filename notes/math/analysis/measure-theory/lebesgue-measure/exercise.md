::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-1-q8 origin="external" source="camii-pms-1-q8"}
Let $B$ be a Borel subset of the interval $[0,1]$. Show that for every $\varepsilon>0$, there exists a finite union of disjoint intervals $A=(a_1,b_1]\cup\dots\cup(a_n,b_n]$ such that the Lebesgue measure of $A\triangle B$ ($=(A^c\cap B)\cup(A\cap B^c)$) is less than $\varepsilon$. Show further that this remains true for every Borel set in $\mathbb R$ of finite Lebesgue measure.
:::
