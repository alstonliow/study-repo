::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-3-q2 origin="external" source="camii-pms-3-q2"}
Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be finite Borel measures on $\mathbb R$. Let $f$ be a continuous bounded function on $\mathbb R$. Show that $f$ is integrable with respect to $\mu$ and $\nu$. Show further that, if $\mu(f)=\nu(f)$ for all such $f$, then $\mu=\nu$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-3-q7 origin="external" source="camii-pms-3-q7"}
Show that, as $n\to\infty$,
$$\int_0^\infty\sin(e^x)/(1+nx^2)\,dx\to0\qquad\text{and}\qquad\int_0^1(n\cos x)/(1+n^2x^{\frac32})\,dx\to0.$$
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-3-q8 origin="external" source="camii-pms-3-q8"}
Let $u$ and $v$ be differentiable functions on $\mathbb R$ with continuous derivatives $u'$ and $v'$. Suppose that $uv'$ and $u'v$ are integrable on $\mathbb R$ and $u(x)v(x)\to0$ as $|x|\to\infty$. Show that
$$\int_{\mathbb R}u(x)v'(x)\,dx=-\int_{\mathbb R}u'(x)v(x)\,dx.$$
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-3-q10 origin="external" source="camii-pms-3-q10"}
The moment generating function $\varphi$ of a real-valued random variable $X$ is defined by $\varphi(\theta)=\mathbb E(e^{\theta X})$, $\theta\in\mathbb R$. Suppose that $\varphi$ is finite on an open interval containing $0$. Show that $\varphi$ has derivatives of all orders at $0$ and that $X$ has finite moments of all orders given by
$$\mathbb E(X^n)=\Big(\frac{d}{d\theta}\Big)^n\Big|_{\theta=0}\varphi(\theta).$$
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-4-q1 origin="external" source="camii-pms-4-q1"}
Let $(f_n:n\in\mathbb N)$ be a sequence of integrable functions and suppose that $f_n\to f$ a.e. for some integrable function $f$. Show that, if $\|f_n\|_1\to\|f\|_1$, then $\|f_n-f\|_1\to0$.
:::
