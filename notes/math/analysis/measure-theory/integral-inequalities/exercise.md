::: {.problem #prob-camii-pms-4-q2 origin="external" source="camii-pms-4-q2"}
Let $X$ be a random variable and let $1\le p<\infty$. Show that, if $X\in L^p(\mathbb P)$, then $\mathbb P(|X|\ge\lambda)=O(\lambda^{-p})$ as $\lambda\to\infty$. Prove the identity
$$\mathbb E(|X|^p)=\int_0^\infty p\lambda^{p-1}\,\mathbb P(|X|\ge\lambda)\,d\lambda$$
and deduce that, for all $q>p$, if $\mathbb P(|X|\ge\lambda)=O(\lambda^{-q})$ as $\lambda\to\infty$, then $X\in L^p(\mathbb P)$.
:::
