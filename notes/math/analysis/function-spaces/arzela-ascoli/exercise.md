::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-3-q13 origin="external" source="camii-lans-3-q13"}
Let $\mathcal O(U)$ denote the space of holomorphic functions on the non-empty open subset $U$ of the complex plane. Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence in $\mathcal O(U)$ that is uniformly bounded on every compact subset of $U$. Show that some subsequence of $(f_n)$ converges locally uniformly on $U$.
:::
