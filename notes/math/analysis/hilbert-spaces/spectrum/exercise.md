::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q4 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q4"}
Let $U$ be a unitary operator on a complex Hilbert space. Show that $\sigma(U)\subset\mathbb T$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q5 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q5"}
Let $H$ be a complex Hilbert space with orthonormal basis $(e_n)_{n=-\infty}^\infty$. The \emph{bilateral shift} is the operator $T$ on $H$ defined by $Te_n=e_{n+1}$ ($n\in\mathbb Z$). Find the spectrum of $T$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q6 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q6"}
Let $K$ be a non-empty compact subset of $\mathbb C$. Show that there is an operator on $\ell_2$ whose spectrum is $K$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q7 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q7"}
Prove that if $T$ is a normal operator then $\sigma(T)=\sigma_{\mathrm{ap}}(T)$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q13 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q13"}
Let $T$ be an operator on a Banach space $X$ with $\|T\|<1$. Show that $I-T$ has a square root, i.e., there exists $S\in\mathcal B(X)$ such that $S^2=I-T$.
:::
