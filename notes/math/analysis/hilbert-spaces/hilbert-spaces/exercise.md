::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-3-q6 origin="external" source="camii-lans-3-q6"}
Let $X$ be an inner product space, and let $T:X\to X$ be a linear map. Show that $\langle Tx,Ty\rangle=\langle x,y\rangle$ for all $x,y\in X$ if and only if $\|Tx\|=\|x\|$ for all $x\in X$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-3-q7 origin="external" source="camii-lans-3-q7"}
Let $X$ be a complex inner product space, and let $T:X\to X$ be a linear map. Show that if $\langle Tx,x\rangle=0$ for all $x\in X$, then $T=0$. Does the same conclusion hold in the real case?
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-3-q8 origin="external" source="camii-lans-3-q8"}
Let $X$ be a normed space. Show that if $\|x+y\|^2+\|x-y\|^2=2\|x\|^2+2\|y\|^2$ for all $x,y\in X$, then $X$ is an inner product space.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-3-q9 origin="external" source="camii-lans-3-q9"}
Construct an inner product space $X$ and a proper closed subspace $Y$ of $X$ such that $Y^\perp=\{0\}$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q11 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q11"}
Let $A=(a_{ij})_{i,j=1}^\infty$ be a complex matrix whose rows and columns form a bounded set in $\ell_2$. Must $A$ be the matrix of a bounded linear operator on $\ell_2$?
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-4-q14 origin="external" source="camii-lans-4-q14"}
Let $X$ be a separable infinite-dimensional Banach space. Suppose that there is a constant $C$ such that every finite-dimensional subspace $E$ of $X$ satisfies $d(E,\ell_2^n)\le C$, where $n=\dim E$. Prove that $X$ is isomorphic to $\ell_2$.
:::
