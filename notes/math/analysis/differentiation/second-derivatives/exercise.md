Consider the function $\det : GL_n(\mathbb{R}) \to \mathbb{R}$, where $GL_n(\mathbb{R}) = \{X \in M_n : X \text{ invertible}\}$.

(a) Show that $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$ is an open subset of $M_n$.

(b) Show that $\det$ is differentiable over all of $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$, with $D\det|_A(H) = \det A\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}H)$.

(c) Show that $\det$ is twice differentiable at $I$ and find $D^2\det|_I$ as a bilinear map.
:::
