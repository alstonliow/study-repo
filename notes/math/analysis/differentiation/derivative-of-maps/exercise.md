($\star$) Let $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ and $(x_0,y_0) \in \mathbb{R}^2$.

(a) Suppose that $\partial_1f$ exists and is continuous in some open ball around $(x_0,y_0)$, and that $\partial_2f$ exists at $(x_0,y_0)$. Show that $f$ is differentiable at $(x_0,y_0)$.

(b) Suppose instead that $\partial_1f$ exists and is bounded on some open ball around $(x_0,y_0)$, and that for fixed $x$ the function $y \mapsto f(x,y)$ is continuous. Show that $f$ is continuous at $(x_0,y_0)$.
:::
