::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q10 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q10"}
Let $X$ be a closed subspace of $\ell_1$. Assume that every $y=(x_{2n})\in\ell_1$ extends to a sequence $x=(x_n)\in X$. Show that there is a constant $C$ such that $x$ can always be chosen to satisfy $\|x\|\le C\|y\|$.
:::
