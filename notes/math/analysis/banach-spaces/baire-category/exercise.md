::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q6 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q6"}
Let $X$ be a non-empty countable complete metric space. Show that $X$ has an isolated point.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q7 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q7"}
Let $Y$ be a proper subspace of a Banach space $X$. Can $Y$ be dense $\mathcal G_\delta$, i.e., can $Y$ be the intersection of a sequence of dense open sets in $X$?
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q8 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q8"}
Let $1\le p<q$. Consider the \emph{subset} $Y=\ell_p$ of the Banach space $X=(\ell_q,\|\cdot\|_q)$. Show that $Y$ is meagre in $X$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q11 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q11"}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be a continuous function such that for every $x>0$ we have $f(nx)\to0$ as $n\to\infty$. Show that $f(x)\to0$ as $x\to\infty$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q13 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q13"}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be a pointwise limit of a sequence of continuous functions. Show that $f$ has a point of continuity.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q14 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q14"}
Let $X$ be a normed space that is homeomorphic to a complete metric space. Prove that $X$ is complete.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q15 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q15"}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be an infinitely differentiable function such that for every $x\in\mathbb R$ there is an $n\in\mathbb N$ with $f^{(m)}(x)=0$ for all $m\ge n$. Prove that $f$ is a polynomial.
:::
