(i) Prove carefully that $C[0,1]$ is incomplete in the $L_1$-norm $\|\cdot\|_1$.

(ii) Show that the space $C^1[0,1]$ is incomplete in the uniform norm $\|\cdot\|_\infty$ but complete in the norm $\|f\|=\|f\|_\infty+\|f'\|_\infty$.

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-1-q3 origin="external" source="camii-lans-1-q3"}
Let $T:\ell_p^n\to\ell_q^n$ be the identity map of the underlying vector space $\mathbb R^n$. Compute the operator norm of $T$ for all possible values of $p$ and $q$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-1-q8 origin="external" source="camii-lans-1-q8"}
Prove that a normed space $X$ is a Banach space if and only if every series $\sum x_n$ in $X$ with $\sum\|x_n\|<\infty$ is convergent.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q1 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q1"}
Let $a=(a_n)\in\ell_\infty$. Define $T:\ell_2\to\ell_2$ by $T(\sum x_ne_n)=\sum a_nx_ne_n$. Prove that $T\in\mathcal B(\ell_2)$ and that $\|T\|=\|a\|_\infty$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q2 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q2"}
Let $f$ be a linear functional on a normed space $X$. Prove that $f$ is continuous if and only if $\ker f$ is closed.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q5 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q5"}
Let $X,Y$ be normed spaces that are dense in Banach spaces $\widetilde X,\widetilde Y$, respectively. Let $T\in\mathcal B(X,Y)$. Show that $T$ extends to a unique $\widetilde T\in\mathcal B(\widetilde X,\widetilde Y)$ with $\|\widetilde T\|=\|T\|$. So we may regard $\mathcal B(X,Y)$ as a subspace of $\mathcal B(\widetilde X,\widetilde Y)$. Is $\mathcal B(X,Y)$ dense in $\mathcal B(\widetilde X,\widetilde Y)$? If $T$ is surjective, must $\widetilde T$ be surjective? If $T$ is injective, must $\widetilde T$ be injective?
:::
