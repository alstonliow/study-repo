::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-1-q13 origin="external" source="camii-lans-1-q13"}
Let $Y$ be a proper closed subspace of a normed space $X$. Is there always a non-zero vector $x\in X$ that is `orthogonal' to $Y$ in the sense that $\|x+y\|\ge\|y\|$ for all $y\in Y$?
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camii-lans-2-q4 origin="external" source="camii-lans-2-q4"}
Assume that $X$ is an infinite-dimensional normed space. Show that there is a sequence $(x_n)$ in the unit ball of $X$ with $\|x_m-x_n\|\ge1$ whenever $m\ne n$. Is it possible to replace $\ge$ by $>$?
:::
