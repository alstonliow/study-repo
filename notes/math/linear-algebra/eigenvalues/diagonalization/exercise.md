::: {.problem #prob-mit1806s10-7-q10 origin="external" source="mit1806s10-7-q10"}
Describe all matrices $S$ that diagonalize this matrix $A$ (find all eigenvectors):
$$A=\begin{bmatrix}4&0\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.$$
Then describe all matrices that diagonalize $A^{-1}$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-mit1806s10-7-q12 origin="external" source="mit1806s10-7-q12"}
The transpose of $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$ is $A^{\mathsf T}=(S^{-1})^{\mathsf T}\Lambda S^{\mathsf T}$. The eigenvectors in $A^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{y}=\lambda\mathbf{y}$ are the columns of that matrix $(S^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$. They are often called *left eigenvectors*. How do you multiply matrices to find this formula for $A$?
$$\text{Sum of rank-1 matrices}\qquad A=S\Lambda S^{-1}=\lambda_1\mathbf{x}_1\mathbf{y}_1^{\mathsf T}+\dots+\lambda_n\mathbf{x}_n\mathbf{y}_n^{\mathsf T}.$$
:::
