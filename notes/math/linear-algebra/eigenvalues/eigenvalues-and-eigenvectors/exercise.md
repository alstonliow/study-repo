::: {.problem #prob-mit1806s10-7-q8 origin="external" source="mit1806s10-7-q8"}
A 3 by 3 matrix $B$ is known to have eigenvalues $0,1,2$. This is information is enough to find three of these (give the answers where possible):

(a) the rank of $B$,

(b) the determinant of $B^{\mathsf T}B$,

(c) the eigenvalues of $B^{\mathsf T}B$,

(d) the eigenvalues of $(B^2+I)^{-1}$.
:::
