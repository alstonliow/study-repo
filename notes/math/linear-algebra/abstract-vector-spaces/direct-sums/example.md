::: {#exm-complements-not-unique .example name="Complements are not unique"}

In $V=\mathbb R^2$ let $U=\langle e_2\rangle$, the vertical axis. Both $W_1=\langle e_1\rangle$ and $W_2=\langle e_1+e_2\rangle$ satisfy $U\cap W_i=0$ and $U+W_i=\mathbb R^2$, so $V=U\oplus W_1=U\oplus W_2$. Every line through $0$ other than $U$ is a complement of $U$; only the dimension of a complement is determined.

:::
