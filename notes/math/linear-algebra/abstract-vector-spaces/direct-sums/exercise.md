::: {.problem #prob-camib-las-1-q8 origin="external" source="camib-las-1-q8"}
Let $U_1, \dots, U_k$ be subspaces of a vector space $V$ and let $B_i$ be a basis for $U_i$. Show that the following statements are equivalent:

(i) $U = \sum_i U_i$ is a direct sum, i.e. every element of $U$ can be written uniquely as $\sum_i u_i$ with $u_i \in U_i$.

(ii) $U_j \cap \sum_{i\ne j} U_i = \{0\}$ for all $j$.

(iii) The $B_i$ are pairwise disjoint and their union is a basis for $\sum_i U_i$.

Give an example where $U_i \cap U_j = \{0\}$ for all $i \ne j$, yet $U_1 + \dots + U_k$ is not a direct sum.
:::
