Let $T$, $U$ and $W$ be subspaces of $V$.

(i) Show that $T \cup U$ is a subspace of $V$ only if either $T \le U$ or $U \le T$.

(ii) Give explicit counter-examples to the following statements:
$$\text{(a)}\quad T + (U \cap W) = (T + U) \cap (T + W); \qquad\qquad \text{(b)}\quad (T + U) \cap W = (T \cap W) + (U \cap W).$$

(iii) Show that each of the equalities in (ii) can be replaced by a valid inclusion of one side in the other.
