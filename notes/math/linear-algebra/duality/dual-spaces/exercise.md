::: {.problem #prob-camib-las-2-q8 origin="external" source="camib-las-2-q8"}
For $A \in \mathbb{M}_{n\times m}(\mathbb{F})$ and $B \in \mathbb{M}_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$, let $\tau_A(B)$ denote $\operatorname{tr}(AB)$. Show that, for each fixed $A$, $\tau_A\colon \mathbb{M}_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F}) \to \mathbb{F}$ is linear. Show moreover that the mapping $A \mapsto \tau_A$ defines a linear isomorphism $\mathbb{M}_{n\times m}(\mathbb{F}) \to \mathbb{M}_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})^*$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camib-las-2-q9 origin="external" source="camib-las-2-q9"}
(a) Suppose that $f \in \mathbb{M}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})^*$ is such that $f(AB) = f(BA)$ for all $A, B \in \mathbb{M}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$ and $f(I) = n$. Show that $f$ is the trace functional, i.e. $f(A) = \operatorname{tr} A$ for all $A \in \mathbb{M}_{n\times n}(\mathbb{R})$.

(b) Now let $V$ be a non-zero finite dimensional real vector space. Show that there are no endomorphisms $\alpha, \beta$ of $V$ with $\alpha\beta - \beta\alpha = \mathrm{id}_V$.

(c) Let $V$ be the space of infinitely differentiable functions $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. Find endomorphisms $\alpha$ and $\beta$ of $V$ such that $\alpha\beta - \beta\alpha = \mathrm{id}_V$.
:::

::: {.problem #prob-camib-las-2-q10 origin="external" source="camib-las-2-q10"}
(a) Let $a_0, \dots, a_n$ be distinct real numbers, and let
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ a_0 & a_1 & \cdots & a_n \\ a_0^2 & a_1^2 & \cdots & a_n^2 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_0^n & a_1^n & \cdots & a_n^n \end{pmatrix}.$$
Show that $\det(A) \ne 0$.

(b) Let $P_n$ be the space of real polynomials of degree at most $n$. For $x \in \mathbb{R}$ define $e_x \in P_n^*$ by $e_x(p) = p(x)$. By considering the standard basis $(1, t, \dots, t^n)$ for $P_n$, use (a) to show that $\{e_0, \dots, e_n\}$ is linearly independent and hence forms a basis for $P_n^*$.

(c) Identify the basis of $P_n$ to which $(e_0, \dots, e_n)$ is dual.
:::
