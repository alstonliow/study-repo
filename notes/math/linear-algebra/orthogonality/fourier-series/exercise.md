$$\int_0^{2\pi}f(x)\cos x\,dx=0\qquad\int_0^{2\pi}f(x)\sin x\,dx=4\qquad\int_0^{2\pi}f(x)\sin2x\,dx=0.$$

*Note (these notes):* The square wave is $f=1$ on $(0,\pi)$ and $f=-1$ on $(\pi,2\pi)$, as in @exm-fourier-square-wave.
