::: {.problem #prob-mit18065ps-18-q10 origin="external" source="mit18065ps-18-q10"}
Show that this $2n\times2n$ KKT matrix $H$ has $n$ positive and $n$ negative eigenvalues :
$$S\text{ positive definite},\quad C\text{ invertible}\qquad H=\begin{bmatrix}S&C\\C^{\mathsf T}&0\end{bmatrix}$$
The first $n$ pivots from $S$ are positive. The last $n$ pivots come from $-C^{\mathsf T}S^{-1}C$.
:::
