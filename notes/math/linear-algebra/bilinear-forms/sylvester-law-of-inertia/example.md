::: {#exm-semidefinite-standard-forms .example name="Semidefinite forms in normal form"}

On $\mathbb R^n$ let $\varphi$ have matrix $\begin{bmatrix}I_p&0\\0&0_{n-p}\end{bmatrix}$. Then $q(x)=x_1^2+\dots+x_p^2\ge0$, so $\varphi$ is positive semidefinite; it is positive definite exactly when $p=n$, since otherwise $q(e_n)=0$. With $-I_p$ in place of $I_p$, $\varphi$ is negative semidefinite, and negative definite exactly when $p=n$.

:::
