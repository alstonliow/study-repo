::: {.problem #prob-mit18065ps-13-q1 origin="external" source="mit18065ps-13-q1"}
Given positive numbers $a_1,\dots,a_n$ find positive numbers $p_1,\dots,p_n$ so that
$$p_1+\dots+p_n=1\quad\text{and}\quad V=\frac{a_1^2}{p_1}+\dots+\frac{a_n^2}{p_n}\ \text{ reaches its minimum } (a_1+\dots+a_n)^2 .$$
The derivatives of $L(\mathbf{p},\lambda)=V-\lambda(p_1+\dots+p_n-1)$ are zero as in equation (8).
:::
