A unit vector $\mathbf{u}(t)$ describes a point moving around on the unit sphere $\mathbf{u}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{u}=1$. Show that the velocity vector $d\mathbf{u}/dt$ is orthogonal to the position : $\mathbf{u}^{\mathsf T}(d\mathbf{u}/dt)=0$.

Find the eigenvalues of $A_3$ and $A_2$ and $A_1$. Show that they are interlacing :
$$A_3=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&1\end{bmatrix}\qquad A_2=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{bmatrix}\qquad A_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\end{bmatrix}$$

::: {.problem #prob-mit18065ps-16-q3 origin="external" source="mit18065ps-16-q3"}
(a) Find the eigenvalues $\lambda_1(t)$ and $\lambda_2(t)$ of $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}+t\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}$.

(b) At $t=0$, find the eigenvectors of $A(0)$ and verify $\dfrac{d\lambda}{dt}=\mathbf{y}^{\mathsf T}\dfrac{dA}{dt}\mathbf{x}$.

(c) Check that the change $A(t)-A(0)$ is positive semidefinite for $t>0$. Then verify the interlacing law $\lambda_1(t)\ge\lambda_1(0)\ge\lambda_2(t)\ge\lambda_2(0)$.
:::

Which $i$ of those eigenvectors are a basis for an $i$-dimensional subspace $Y$ with this property : The minimum of $\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf T}S\mathbf{x}/\mathbf{x}^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{x}$ for $\mathbf{x}$ in $Y$ is $\lambda_i$.
