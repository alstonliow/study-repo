::: {#exm-determinant-small-n .example name="The permutation formula for n = 2, 3"}

1. $S_2=\{\mathrm{id},(1\,2)\}$, with signs $+1,-1$, so $\det A=A_{11}A_{22}-A_{12}A_{21}$.

2. $S_3$ has three even permutations ($\mathrm{id}$ and the two $3$-cycles) and three transpositions, so
$$\det A=A_{11}A_{22}A_{33}+A_{12}A_{23}A_{31}+A_{13}A_{21}A_{32}-A_{11}A_{23}A_{32}-A_{12}A_{21}A_{33}-A_{13}A_{22}A_{31}.$$

:::
