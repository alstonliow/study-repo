::: {.problem #prob-mit1806s10-3-q7 origin="external" source="mit1806s10-3-q7"}
Suppose $R$ is $m$ by $n$ of rank $r$, with pivot columns first:
$$R=\begin{bmatrix}I&F\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$

(a) What are the shapes of those four blocks?

(b) Find a right-inverse $B$ with $RB=I$ if $r=m$.

(c) Find a left-inverse $C$ with $CR=I$ if $r=n$.

(d) What is the reduced row echelon form of $R^{\mathsf T}$ (with shapes)?

(e) What is the reduced row echelon form of $R^{\mathsf T}R$ (with shapes)?

Prove that $R^{\mathsf T}R$ has the same nullspace as $R$. Later we show that $A^{\mathsf T}A$ always has the same nullspace as $A$ (a valuable fact).
:::
